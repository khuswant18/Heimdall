Heimdall capture-gap tester

Faithful replica of Heimdall 1.2.9's screen capture: one JPEG at quality 0.2 every 15 seconds (SNAPSHOT_INTERVAL_MS = 15000). It records nothing between frames — exactly like the real app. Use it to measure your personal blind window: start capture, then press M the instant you do a "cheat" action and M again when you hide it. If no captured frame's timestamp lands inside that window, the real proctor would have no visual evidence either.
